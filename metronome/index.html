Meter and tempo notes ⌄

In simple meters the BPM counts the denominator note (for example, ♩ in 4/4). In 6/8, 9/8 and 12/8 it counts the dotted-quarter pulse. In 5/8 and 7/8 it counts eighth notes, with secondary accents at group starts. Switch on Subdivisions to hear the smaller notes between main beats. Italian markings describe approximate, overlapping ranges rather than fixed speeds. Tap tempo averages your recent taps. Audio begins after you press Start, as required by mobile browsers.
