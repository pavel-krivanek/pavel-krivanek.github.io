How the adaptive difficulty works

The interval is measured in 12-tone equal temperament. The second note is shifted by a random number of cents. Within the in-tune window, both “In tune” and the physically correct direction are accepted. Three correct answers in a row narrow the error range and the in-tune window. Replay doesn't change your score.
