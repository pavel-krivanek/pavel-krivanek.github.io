Using MDOS

The supplied D80 image is inserted into drive A at startup. MDOS initializes automatically after reset; enter disk commands directly in BASIC. SNAP saves the complete current machine state to the active writable disk as SNAPSHOT00 through SNAPSHOT99, then resumes the program.

Shift paper

















The 8255 is addressed at 1Fh (A), 3Fh (B), 5Fh (C) and 7Fh (control). Choose the wiring expected by the printer driver: the Didaktik connection reads status on A and writes control on B; the UR-4 connection shares port C, with its lower nibble driving the printer and upper nibble returning sensors.

Enable the interface, then click the emulator screen to grab the pointer. Press Esc to release it.




The interface exposes the standard Kempston mouse counters on FBDFh and FFDFh, with active-low buttons on FADFh. Coordinates wrap from 255 to 0 like the hardware counters.
